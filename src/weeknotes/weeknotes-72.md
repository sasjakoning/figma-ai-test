## Highlights

Last Saturday I went to see Sophie Straat with friends and had a really good time. She had a song where (controversially,) white men were asked to step back so all women and minorities could do a mosh pit. It was the first mosh pit I've ever done and had a ton of fun.

The artist has been getting a lot of pushback over that move, going as far as her getting death treats. There's a lot of nuance to this that I won't get into but personally, I think it's fine for white men to step back for once, and have everyone else take the spotlight for a while.



On Wednesday I went to the Drawclub where we sat outside and listened to the church next to us make noise for an hour. I didn't feel like drawing (+ my iPad wasn't charged) so I worked on my website instead.



Today (Friday) I'm going out for dinner with friends and going to see The Odyssey so I look forward to that.

I also look forward to tomorrow, I'm going to Amsterdam with my grandma, which we've been trying to plan for nearly a year but keep forgetting to.
