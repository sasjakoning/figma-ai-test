## Consumed

- [Pluribus](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt22202452/?ref_=nv_sr_srsg_0_tt_6_nm_2_in_0_q_plurib): Started watching this and might have watched all five (up till now) episodes in a single sitting... Wow I love this show?? Spoilers: When you look at the premise of this show you might think, meh, another end of the world show/movie but it's actually really good. I love the main character AND love that the show keeps most of the focus on her as well. Also, I really like how emotional the "hive mind" is. Them freaking out when Carol expresses strong emotions towards them, them giving her the silent treatment when she's mistreated them too much... Love it. I wonder how much of the way the hive mind acts is inspired by Large Language Models and the way they also try to do everything to make the user happy, even when it might be dangerous. And in that case I love that it seems to be so inspired by AI while it's not doing anything involving it at all. (5/5)
- [The Running Man](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt14107334/?ref_=nv_sr_srsg_2_tt_5_nm_1_in_0_q_the%2520running%2520man): typical action movie. Enjoyable enough! (3/5)
