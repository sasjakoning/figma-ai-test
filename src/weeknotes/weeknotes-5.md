## Consumed

- [Severance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Severance_(TV_series)): I finished watching Severance, and I would say it's a solid 5/5 stars for me. I loved the whole mystery and the characters. It's not often that I watch a show and like all of the characters; they're really fleshed out! I also liked that the ending wasn't a HUGE cliffhanger. Of course it set things up for the next season, but they answered a lot of questions as well. Really curious to see where it goes from here.
- [The Wheel Of Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wheel_of_Time_(TV_series)): My colleagues pushed me to start watching The Wheel Of Time (they had some pretty convincing arguments...). I just finished watching season one, and it's pretty good. I'm not very attached to the main 5 characters yet, but there are some others I like. When it comes to the story, I was thrown off a little. I thought reaching their goal and figuring out who the "dragon reborn" is would take several seasons, but they managed to do it in one season. It really makes me wonder what's planned for the next seasons. I'll find out next week.
