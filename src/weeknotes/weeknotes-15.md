## Highlights

So last week I mentioned going to the theatre, and I did. We went to see[ Karin Bloemen](https://labloemen.nl/), who is a Dutch actress and singer. She did a show talking about a bunch of stuff and singing some songs. Even though I don't know her very well, it was really enjoyable. The average age of the audience must've been over 60, though, as there were a lot of jokes and songs about their youth that didn't always quite land for me. Nevertheless, it was fun.

Last week my mom and I also (finally) booked our holiday to Iceland. I've had it on my mind for a while since my colleagues were really positive about it, and my mom has always wanted to see the northern lights. It was a little challenging since we decided to book everything on our own, which meant going on [Booking.com](http://booking.com) day by day to pick out hotels and cabins. [This site](https://www.reisjunk.nl/ijsland/reisroute-ijsland-tips/) was a lifesaver, though, as it gave us great tips for our trip. We're going for 8 days in February next year, so I'm really looking forward to it.

- [Mission: Impossible - The Final Reckoning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mission:_Impossible_(film_series)): So, I've seen some of the movies before this one but never really paid much attention to them.
