Your goal is to climb to the top of a large mountain, where you have to control each limb of your character individually and also manage stamina, health, hunger and so on. I had a great time playing this. It took me about 12 hours to get to the top on my first run and I might play again as more routers are possible + a DLC is coming. 10/10
