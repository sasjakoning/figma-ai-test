## Consumed

- [Killing Eve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_Eve): So nearly a year ago I started watching this show, already knowing how it ends, since the internet had an outrage over it. Either way I watched it until I got to somewhere in season 4 (last season) where I dropped off a little. Last week I watched the remaining few episodes and yeah... the ending is REALLY BAD. I fully understand why so many people are mad about it. It's a shame because the rest of the show is pretty good. I don't know how to rate this.
- [Black Swan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Swan_(film)): Another movie ticket off my watch list. I kind of already knew where this movie was going, and still I was pretty stressed throughout. I think I'm learning that I'm not a huge fan of psychosis movies because I couldn't get through Beau Is Afraid either. Anyway, (3/5).
- [Wednesday Season 2 part 1](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt13443470/): I loved loved season 1 so I was excited to watch season 2. Though honestly, I'm not entirely sure yet. Spoiler warning - I felt like they were trying to fit too many storylines into 4 episodes. There's the stalker girl thing, then the stalker birds, and the vision of Enid. Two of them have been resolved now so I really hope the remaining 4 episodes will be more focused. Also, I really expected there to be more focus on the dynamic between Wednesday and Enid, especially since Wednesday's main goal this season is to save Enid. But she had a surprisingly small amount of screen time? + I'm not a big fan of the romance between Enid and Bruno. Who the fuck is Bruno? ... You can tell I'm passionate about this.
