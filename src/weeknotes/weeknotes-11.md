## Consumed

- [The Last Of Us (HBO series)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_of_Us_(TV_series)) : Another episode released (E4). This one was really enjoyable. There were some really tense action scenes which were planned out really well, and Dina and Ellie finally confessed to eachother... In a way. They're going in quite a different direction with their relationship compared to the game which is interesting!
- [Sinners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinners_(2025_film)): Watched with one with my friend last week on Saturday. Pretty good! It's a vampire movie but the setting and theme are a little different compared to what you usually see. (4/5)
- [Thunderbolts*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thunderbolts*): Watched this one on Saturday. Had a pretty good time during this even though it was VERY corny. But hey, I'd watch anything if Florence Pugh is in it... (3,5/5)
- [Clair Obscur - Expedition 33](https://www.expedition33.com/): Started playing this game last week and have kind of been addicted ever since. The mechanics are kind of a mix between Bladurs Gate III (Turn based combat) and Dark Souls (Timing dodges/parries) which is really fun. Along with that the story is good and it's made by a smaller studio which I prefer... And Jennifer English voices one of the main characters.
