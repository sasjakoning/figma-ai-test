## Highlights

Last week was a little more eventful than the previous one. On Wednesday I had another Drawclub session in which we did some "[Urban Sketching](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_Sketchers)". We started at our usual location and then walked around the city a bit to draw in different areas. The weather was really nice, and there were quite a few people present this time, so it was enjoyable.

This Saturday I went to another fantasy fair, this time hosted at [Archeon](https://www.archeon.nl/index.html). I've been there before during the winter for a similar event and really wanted to go to this one as well. And just like the last time, I had a ton of fun. There was live music and shows such as knights fighting on horses and reenactments. The weather was nice, but very hot. It ended up being over 30 degrees, so everyone was avoiding the sun and being sweaty. I'd totally go again, though.
