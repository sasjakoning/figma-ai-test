## Highlights

The past week has been a bit of a tough one. At work we use Figma a lot, and I've been busy reworking our entire design system to use the new native slot feature. Well, turns out that feature has a bug that impacts our workflow, which we didn't discover until now. It causes content in slots to shift when we click on it. This can happen to designed pages without us being aware of it, and cause the developers to design something broken. That issue drained me mentally, as I feel responsible for it.

I managed to isolate the bug though, and am in contact with Figma to get it resolved. I hope it's fixed soon but for now there's a shitty workaround at least.

Besides that on Thursday evening my cat pranked me by "trying to pee" in the litter box, which he usually just does outside, but nothing seemed to come out. Male cats not being able to pee is life threatening and requires immediate attention. So yeah, I was stressed the hell out. But I didn't want to call the emergency vet right away, as he was acting normally besides that.

I kept him inside all night though which meant he was restless as fuck and so was I because I wanted to see him try peeing again to confirm the issue.

Around 6 am he finally peed without trouble. Night wasted.

I think I'll schedule an appointment anyway to do a general check-up.
