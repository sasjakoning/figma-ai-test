## Consumed

- [Severance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Severance_(TV_series)): Started watching the show Severance. I think I'm on episode three right now, and it's been enjoyable so far. I like that it's seemingly smaller scale, two main locations, a small amount of characters to focus on, and a clear premise.
- [R.E.P.O.](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3241660/REPO/): Last weekend I played R.E.P.O. with some friends, which was surprising enjoyable. When you see the logo of the game, you imagine it's just some simple meme game, but it's actually quite detailed and incredibly fun.
- [Hell Let Loose](https://www.hellletloose.com/): I started playing some HHL again after taking a break for a couple of months. I really enjoy the "realism" of the game, and the community around it has been nothing but kind. I highly recommend!
