## Highlights

Last Saturday I went to a large vintage market in Amsterdam with a friend. We had been there before, last year, and had a pretty good time so we decided to go again. This time we didn't find as much good stuff though. But I bought two matching fleece sweaters for my mom and I which I found funny.

On Sunday my mom and I went to ZuiderzeeLicht, which is a seasonal event hosted at the [Zuiderzeemuseum](https://www.zuiderzeemuseum.nl/) in Enkuizen. Every year they tell a local story using different sets and Immersive Environments. This year there was a pretty good layer of snow which really added to the experience. I love walking around there when it's dark, experience the story and close off the night with some hot cocoa.

It's pretty funny and I'm enjoying the dynamics between the two actors.
