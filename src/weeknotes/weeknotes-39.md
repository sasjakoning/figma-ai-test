## Highlights

Last week on Monday my mom and I went to [Wende](https://wende.nu/) in Het Concertgebouw in Amsterdam. I've known Wende's music for a while and when I saw that there were two seats available in her otherwise sold out show, I took the opportunity. And turns out it was worth it as the show was really good. She sang some of her own songs along with songs from other artists with some nice lighting effects and beautiful vocals. Typhoon also sang a song or two with her as a guest.

Besides that, on Thursday we had a game night at work with some colleagues where I played [Dwellings of Eldervale](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/271055/dwellings-of-eldervale) for the first time which turns out to be a pretty fun game though a bit complex.
