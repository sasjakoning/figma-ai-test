## Highlights

Last week on Monday I arrived back from my holiday to Ireland. I figured I'd be quite worn out from the holiday so I had taken the rest of the week off as well which ended up being a good idea. I mainly just relaxed, played some games and went places.

On Thursday, I went to The Hague to get my feet scanned for figure out what my sizing is for new ice skates. I tried on a bunch of skates and ended up with the Bauer Supreme M30's which I'm quite happy with. The skating rink won't be open until late September, which means I won't be able to break them in on the ice. So instead, I've been wearing them at home with blade covers to get them ready. The circulation in my feet hasn't been cut off yet by them, which is a good sign. My previous skates did have that issue.

Last week I also bought a record player since I ordered Laufey's new album + an art card with her signature. It wouldn't be right to have a vinyl without a player... I may also have bought a couple more records already as well... such as AURORA's What Happened To The Heart, Chappell Roan's The Rise And Fall Of A Midwest Princess, and Jeff Wayne's Musical Version of The War of the Worlds (!!! Which was such a lucky find! I love that album!).
