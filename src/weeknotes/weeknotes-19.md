On Sunday I went to the Formula 1 Exhibition with my parents, brother, and nephew. It was my dad's birthday a while ago, and since he's a pretty big Formula 1 fan, we thought it'd be nice to go there with the family.

Even though my obsession with F1 has cooled down to a simmer, it was an enjoyable experience. The exhibition was set up nicely, though it lacked a bit of interactivity. It was really interesting to see Grosjean's cockpit from his crash, though.
