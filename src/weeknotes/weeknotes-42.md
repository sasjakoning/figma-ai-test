## Highlights

First off, I wrote a bit about piracy last week and turned it into a blogpost you can read [here](https://sasjakoning.com/blog/piracy-is-returning-and-i-have-thoughts).

Last week on Friday I celebrated Sinterklaas with my family which was enjoyable. Among other things, I was gifted two records, Led Zeppelin IV and the Minecraft (Game, not movie) soundtrack which I'm really happy with.

The day after I went to the Archeon Winterfair with a friend. Last year I went as well which was the first time I had ever gone to an event like that. While the events at Archeon are of smaller scale, the setting is amazing and in general I just love the vibe of the people there. We joined to Balfolk workshops in which we learned some traditional dances. I wasn't much into dancing but this workshop really changed my mind. I might take some real lessons in Balfolk sometime, if I feel like doing a side quest. I didn't really take any pictures this time though... Guess we were content with just enjoying ourselves.
