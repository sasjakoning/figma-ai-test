## Highlights

Last week has been decently productive.

On Wednesday I went to the Drawclub, where I made some small illustrations for a new blogpost, but mostly just ended up chatting a whole lot.

Thursday I went to [Mitski](https://open.spotify.com/artist/2uYWxilOVlUdk4oV9DvwqK?si=zT9ft5Y2TPShdilDjQUXIA) with a friend where I had a pretty good time. Her live voice was nice and I was surprised by how quirky she is irl. Bought a long-sleeve with her latest album on it.

This week on a whim I bought a LoRa board that I've been playing around it. It's basically a way to communicate with people using radio signals, so you don't need internet or satellites. It's pretty cool and I'm learning a lot from it. Running into some blockers though, but I'm sure I can get past those.

I'll be writing a post about that as well, though I'm afraid my list of things to do is growing a bit too quick. I'm still working on a post about the Fediverse, and have an ongoing project with my 433Mhz controlled gate. Along with that I'm also researching integrating Matter with my Home Assistant, which is an alternative to Zigbee.

So yeah, plenty of stuff to do with only two days per week to tinker.
