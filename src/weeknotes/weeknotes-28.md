## Highlights

Another week, another weeknotes...

Last weekend on Saturday, I went to a record market in Hoorn with my grandma. Apparently, they host this twice a year where they set up many stands selling records, CDs, cassettes, and more. My parents were on holiday the entire week, so I took my grandma with me since she enjoys going out with family. In hindsight, maybe I should've brought a wheelchair, though, since she had some trouble keeping up (Or, I had trouble keeping up with her). Either way, it was an enjoyable afternoon. I bought Exile on Main St. by The Rolling Stones, which is my (other) grandma's favourite album, and I almost bought the Wicked movie soundtrack, but I was strong enough to resist (Or I want to buy both movie soundtracks once Wicked: For Good releases...).

Besides going out that weekend, the rest of the week was decently uneventful. Since my parents were away, I stayed mostly at home except for work to keep our dog company. On Wednesday I did go to the Draw Club where I doodled some stuff, but again I forgot to export the drawings.

Coming Sunday I'm going to an artists market in Hoorn with the Draw Club that I'm looking forward to.

## Consumed

- [Wednesday Season 2 Part 2](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt13443470/): The second (and last) part of Wednesday Season 2 released. I've got to say, it did improve in the second part compared to the first part. There were certainly parts I wasn't interested in, such as the whole thing going on the zombie, but I generally enjoyed myself and it got some laughs out of me. Spoiler warning: There were rumours in advance that there would be a body swap episode which I wasn't excited about, but it actually turned out to be my favourite episode. Emma and Jenna are such good actors. I honestly thought they dubbed each other's voices, but turns out they just mimicked them really well.
